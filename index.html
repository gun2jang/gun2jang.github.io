About

I am a senior undergraduate in the Department of Data Science at Korea University and a research intern at PRISM Lab, advised by Prof. Hyungjin Chung.

My current focus is on using generative priors to infer molecular structures from cryo-electron microscopy (cryo-EM) density maps. More broadly, I am interested in how measurement models, sampling dynamics, and computation shape the behavior of generative inverse problem solvers.

I have also worked on efficient long-context inference, neural operators, and single-cell perturbation modeling. I enjoy connecting mathematical analysis with careful experiments and systems implementation.
